GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? We've got you.

A few quick answers to help you get started with Pixla Gold.

Learn about Pixla Gold ↗
What is Pixla Gold? ＋

Pixla Gold is a creator-focused concept platform where users discover content, explore rewards, and learn how eligible coins could be redeemed for selected products.

How can I earn coins? ＋

In this frontend demo, the coin balance is illustrative. A live platform would define eligible activities, verification rules, limits, and reward policies.

Can I redeem coins for real jewellery? ＋

This prototype demonstrates the browsing experience only. Real fulfilment would require a secure backend, verified product inventory, transparent charges, and an operational redemption service.

Is the digital gold price live? ＋

No. No live gold price or financial transaction is connected in this frontend prototype.
