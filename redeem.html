REDEMPTION, EXPLAINED

Clear answers.
No surprises.

Are these real-time prices? ＋

No. The catalogue is a visual demo with sample coin values, not live gold pricing or an offer to sell financial products.

What happens when I click Redeem? ＋

A demo redemption request is saved locally in this browser and shown in your profile. It is not sent to a seller and does not create a real order.

Will my coins sync across devices? ＋

Not in this frontend-only version. Cross-device balances and secure transactions require a backend and database.
